[Text 6] (type=AUTO_SHAPE (1)):

    - • Disjoint Architecture: Standalone ASV and Countermeasure (CM) modules operate in isolation without joint score fusion or cross-risk calibration.
    - • Absence of Dynamic Liveness: Static passphrases fail completely against recorded audio playback, live replay, and responsive voice generation.

    - • Attentive Statistical Pooling (ASP): Weights temporal frames into a 192-d mean & standard deviation vector.
